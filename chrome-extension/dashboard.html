Phishing Awareness Tips

Check Sender Addresses
Always verify the full sender email address. Phishing emails often use domains that closely resemble legitimate ones (e.g., "paypa1.com" instead of "paypal.com").
Beware of Urgency
Phishing emails create a false sense of urgency — "act now," "account suspended," "verify immediately." Legitimate companies rarely pressure you with deadlines.
Hover Before You Click
Always hover over links to preview the actual URL. If it looks unfamiliar, contains IP addresses, or has suspicious subdomains, don't click it.
Never Share Credentials
No legitimate service will ask for your password, PIN, or security codes via email. If asked, it's almost certainly a phishing attempt.
